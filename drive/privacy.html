Privacy

Bookshelf is a reader for PDFs and EPUBs on a Samsung TV, with this page for adding books. It has no servers of its own. Your books stay in your Google Drive.

What Bookshelf can see

Bookshelf signs in with Google using one permission: See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app. It sees only the files it created, such as books you uploaded here, and files you chose yourself in the Google Drive picker. It can’t see the rest of your Drive.

What it stores, and where

  • In your Google Drive: a Bookshelf folder with your uploaded books, their covers, and a small file with your reading progress.
  • On your TV: copies of your books, so they open quickly and work offline, and the sign-in that keeps the TV connected. Signing out on the TV removes both.
  • In this browser tab: the Google sign-in, for up to an hour. Closing the tab removes it.

A book’s title, author and cover are read in your browser when you add it. Nothing is sent anywhere except to your own Google Drive.

What it doesn’t do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data. Bookshelf never sends your books or account details to anyone.

If you run the optional Bookshelf library on your own computer, the TV app sends it short technical logs over your home network. They stay on that computer.

Google

Signing in and the Drive picker are provided by Google, under Google’s privacy policy. Bookshelf’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Remove Bookshelf from your Google account’s connections at any time. Your books stay in the Bookshelf folder of your Drive until you delete them.

Questions

Write to the support email shown on Google’s sign-in screen for Bookshelf.
